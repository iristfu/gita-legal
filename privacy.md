# Your Friend, Gita: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 25, 2026._

## The short version

- To reply, your messages are sent to an AI model provider (Anthropic). Nobody at Your Friend, Gita reads your conversations.
- We don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and we don't use your conversations to train AI models. Anthropic's commercial terms don't allow it to train on them either.
- Your chats are saved on our servers, encrypted, so you can come back to them. You can turn saving off or delete your chats at any time in the app's Settings.
- You don't need to give us your name, email or phone number to use Gita. If you join the waitlist on our website, we keep your email so we can write to you about the launch.
- Subscriptions are handled by Apple. We never see your card or payment details.

## Who we are

Your Friend, Gita is run by Iris Fu, Stanford, California, United States. Contact: hello@yourfriendgita.com. For India, our grievance officer is Iris Fu (hello@yourfriendgita.com).

## What we collect, and why

**On your phone only (never sent to us):**
- Your settings: your reply language, Gita's voice, how Gita talks to you, and whether you've finished the intro.
- What you picked as your goal in the intro, your daily quiz answers, and your reminder time.
- Whether you've turned saving chats off.
- A list of your saved chats' titles (the first words of each chat) and when each was last used, so the chats menu opens instantly. The messages themselves aren't kept on your phone. Deleting a chat, deleting all your chats, or deleting the app removes them.

**Sent to our servers when you chat:**
- **Voice recordings (voice mode only).** When you hold the mic button, your recording is sent to our server and on to our speech-to-text provider (currently Deepgram, with Sarvam AI as a backup) to turn it into text. We don't keep recordings: the app deletes each one after sending it, and our server never stores it. The text of what you said is treated like a typed message, and Gita's spoken reply is generated from the reply text by ElevenLabs (or Cartesia, for some voices).
- **An anonymous account ID.** The app creates one automatically the first time you chat, so your chats and progress belong to you. It isn't linked to your name, email or phone number.
- **Your message and the last few turns of the conversation.** These are used to write Gita's reply. Unless you've turned saving off, your message and Gita's reply are then saved to your chats (see below). Our hosting provider may keep basic technical logs (time, IP address, errors) for a short period; we don't log message content.
- **Your settings for that reply:** reply language, how Gita talks to you, and your device's region (for example "US" or "IN").

**What we keep on our servers:**
- **Your saved chats** (unless you turn saving off): what you wrote or said (as text, never the recording), Gita's replies and the verses they cite. They're tied to your anonymous account ID and encrypted with our own key on top of our hosting provider's encryption, so they can't be read from the database itself. We use them only to show you your chats. We don't read them, analyze them or use them to train AI. They're kept until you delete them, in the app (one chat, or all of them in Settings), or by asking us.
- **Your verse journey:** which verses you've met (cited in a reply to you, or read or listened to in the app) and when, so the app can show the verses you've explored. It holds verse numbers only, never message text, and is tied to your anonymous account ID. Deleting your account deletes it.
- **What Gita remembers** (unless you turn it off in Settings): a few short notes from your chats, such as what you're working through, what you're curious about in the Gita, how you like Gita to talk, and verses that meant something to you, plus a verse and a few questions picked from them. After each reply, an AI model reads that exchange and the current notes and updates them. It's told never to keep anything about health, crises, caste, religion, sexuality, politics, money or other people's private lives, and we filter out notes that mention these. The notes are used only to write Gita's replies and to pick verses and questions for you. They're tied to your anonymous account ID, encrypted with our own key like your saved chats, and never used to train AI. You can see every note, delete any of them, or forget them all in the app (Settings › What Gita remembers); turning it off stops both learning and using them.
- **Your practice log** (unless you turn saving off): which days you meditated or finished the quiz, and for how many minutes. Counts only, tied to your anonymous account ID, so your streak and progress survive a new phone.
- A daily count of how many messages and minutes of voice each anonymous account used, and our total daily AI cost, so we can see what running Gita costs.

**If you join the waitlist on our website:**
- **What we keep:** your email, your phone type and your reason for joining if you choose them, the site or link that brought you to us, and the date. Joining is optional and you don't need the app to do it.
- **Why:** to email you about the app's launch and our Kickstarter. We don't use it for anything else.
- **How long:** until you ask us to remove you, at hello@yourfriendgita.com.
- **Separate from the app:** we don't link it to your anonymous account ID or to anything you do in the app.
- **Your IP address** is checked briefly in our server's memory to stop repeated sign-ups. We don't save it with your sign-up.

## Who processes your data for us

- **Anthropic** (AI model provider, USA) receives your messages to generate Gita's replies, under its commercial terms. Anthropic automatically deletes API inputs and outputs from its systems within 30 days, except where longer storage is needed to enforce its usage policy or is required by law. See [Anthropic's retention policy](https://privacy.claude.com/en/articles/7996866-how-long-do-you-store-my-organization-s-data).
- **Supabase** (database and server hosting, United States, Oregon region) runs our servers and stores your saved chats (encrypted), the counts above and the waitlist.
- **Deepgram** (speech-to-text, USA), with **Sarvam AI** (India) as a backup, turns your voice recordings into text when you use voice.
- **ElevenLabs** and **Cartesia** (text-to-speech, USA) turn Gita's replies into spoken audio. They receive the reply text only.
- **Apple** (App Store) handles subscriptions and payment, and distribution. Apple may share crash reports or feedback you choose to send.
- **RevenueCat** (subscription management, USA) receives your purchase history from Apple and a random ID made by the app, so the app knows whether your trial or plan is active. It doesn't receive your name, email, messages or payment details.
- **GitHub** (website hosting, USA) and **Google Fonts** (USA) serve our website and its fonts, so they receive your IP address and basic browser details when you visit it.

We don't share your data with anyone else except when required by law.

## Subscriptions

Gita is a paid subscription (weekly, monthly or yearly) that starts with a free trial. You buy, manage and cancel it through your Apple account (Settings › your name › Subscriptions). It renews automatically until you cancel. We learn only whether your subscription is active, which plan, and when it renews or ends.

## Not an emergency service

Gita is not an emergency service and not a doctor. If you're in danger, call your local emergency number.

## Your choices and rights

- **Delete your data:** you can delete any chat, or all of them, in the app. Deleting the app removes everything stored on your phone, but not your saved chats: delete them in the app first, or email hello@yourfriendgita.com to have your chats, practice log and the usage counts tied to your account deleted. Deleting the app doesn't cancel a subscription; cancel it in your Apple account.
- **Leave the waitlist:** email hello@yourfriendgita.com and we'll delete your sign-up.
- **Access and correction:** you can ask what we hold about you, and to correct or erase it, at hello@yourfriendgita.com. We'll reply within 30 days.
- **India (Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023):** by using Gita you consent to the processing described here for these purposes only. You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting the app and asking us to erase your data, or by asking us to remove you from the waitlist. You can raise a grievance with our grievance officer at hello@yourfriendgita.com; if you're not satisfied, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
- **US:** we don't sell or "share" personal information for advertising. Residents of states with privacy laws (such as California) can make the requests above at hello@yourfriendgita.com; we won't treat you differently for doing so.

## Children

Parents can contact hello@yourfriendgita.com about a child's data, and we'll delete anything we hold about them.

## Security

Connections between the app and our servers are encrypted (HTTPS). Data on our servers is encrypted at rest by our hosting provider, and saved chats and what Gita remembers are also encrypted with our own key (AES-256-GCM) before they're stored. Server keys are never stored in the app.
